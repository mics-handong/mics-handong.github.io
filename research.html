Research

We work on the circuits that generate and measure time. Clock synthesis and time-to-digital conversion sit underneath almost every modern SoC, and their jitter budgets keep tightening while process variation gets harder to predict. Our answer is digital-heavy architectures, calibration that runs after fabrication, and machine-learning models that shorten the path from a specification to a working design point.
